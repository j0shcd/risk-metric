# Separate registered validation from exploratory research

The evaluation system will have two evidence tiers. The Validation Benchmark contains frozen Registered Hypotheses whose indicator, outcome, expected direction, horizon, statistic, policy, and pass criteria are declared before a run; it alone can support product claims. A separate Exploratory Sweep may search broadly across outcomes, regimes, thresholds, components, ablations, and parameters, but a discovery can enter validation only as a hypothesis frozen in a later evaluation version and cannot promote a claim from the run that discovered it.

## Consequences

- The primary registered evidence centers on whether lower DCA Risk identifies favorable approximately six-month accumulation conditions, whether higher DCA Risk identifies 6–12 month de-risking conditions, and whether a frozen Dynamic DCA policy improves a predeclared investor objective versus identical-cashflow Fixed DCA within explicit guardrails.
- Component metrics are registered only against economically intended supporting outcomes; they explain the aggregate indicator but cannot substitute for failure of the DCA Risk Indicator.
- The existing metric-by-label Cartesian evaluation becomes exploratory and must be excluded from claim promotion.
- Evaluation versions, hypothesis freeze times, model freeze times, and post-freeze observations must be recorded.
